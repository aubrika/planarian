Motion prototypes

Loading: more pronounced breathing and a small tail flex. Working: a counterclockwise contraction around the body. The head stays almost still. Open the interactive motion prototype to pause, change speed, and compare light and dark backgrounds. Animated SVGs include a static reduced-motion treatment.
